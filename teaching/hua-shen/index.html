Hua Shen

Professor • Ph.D.

Hua Shen is a Professor and Master's Supervisor in the Institute of Cyberspace Security at HBUT. The faculty profile records a Ph.D. from Wuhan University (2014), a visiting scholarship at the University of Wollongong, senior membership of the China Computer Federation, and IEEE membership.

Research and Supervision

AI security, information security, privacy-preserving computation and privacy protection. The profile lists supervision in information security, privacy computing and privacy protection for Computer Science and Technology / Electronic Information.

Teaching

Provable Security; Data Structures; Operating System Principles and Security; Cryptography Principles and Applications; Industrial Big Data Security and Privacy Protection.

Selected Projects

  • NSFC Young Scientists Fund: privacy-preserving data aggregation for massive-data analysis.
  • Hubei Provincial Department of Education key project: privacy-protection mechanisms for smart grids.
  • HBUT Green Industry Technology Leading Program: privacy protection for industrial big data published by small and medium-sized enterprises.
