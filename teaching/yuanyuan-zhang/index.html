Yuanyuan Zhang

Associate Professor · Master's Supervisor

Yuanyuan Zhang is an Associate Professor and Master's Supervisor at HBUT and a Nanhu Scholar. The faculty profile reports more than ten SCI papers as first or corresponding author and more than ten patent applications as first applicant.

Research

Public-key cryptography; big-data security and privacy protection; digital economy and AI security; blockchain and industrial Internet security.

Teaching

Mathematical Foundations of Information Security; Discrete Mathematics.

Selected Projects and Recognition

  • Principal investigator, NSFC Young Scientists Fund project on leakage-resilient authenticated key exchange.
  • Second Prize, Science and Technology Award of the China Institute of Communications; co-chair of ProvSec 2023.
  • Member of the Hubei Cyberspace Security Society.
