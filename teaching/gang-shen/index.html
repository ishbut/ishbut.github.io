Team/Gang Shen

Gang Shen

Lecturer · Master's Supervisor

Gang Shen is a Lecturer and Master's Supervisor at HBUT. The faculty profile records a doctoral degree and reports more than 20 SCI/EI-indexed papers and over 10 granted invention patents.

Research and Supervision

Cryptography, and data security and privacy protection for vehicular networks, healthcare networks and artificial intelligence.

Selected Projects

  • Hubei Provincial Natural Science Foundation general project (2023AFB951): key techniques for leakage-resilient homomorphic secret sharing, 2023–2025.
  • Hubei Provincial Department of Education project: security of trustworthy federated learning in smart healthcare, 2024–2026.
