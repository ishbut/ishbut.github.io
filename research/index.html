RESEARCH

Group faculty and students work across four complementary areas:

  • Public-key and applied cryptography: functional and witness encryption, leakage resilience, and program obfuscation.
  • Big-data security and privacy: secure data sharing, verifiable statistics, and outsourced computation.
  • Blockchain and industrial Internet security: blockchain supervision, secure crowdsensing, and IoT privacy.
  • Artificial intelligence security: privacy-preserving federated learning and secure model evaluation.

Recent Research Outputs

  • AI security: adversarial attacks on black-box face recognition (IEEE TIFS, 2026).
  • Secure collaboration: multi-party signatures for vehicular networks (IEEE TVT, 2026) and multi-key homomorphic encryption for federated learning (Information Sciences, 2026).
  • Privacy-preserving data analysis: encrypted graph queries (IEEE TIFS, 2025) and vertically federated optimization (IEEE TIFS, 2026).
