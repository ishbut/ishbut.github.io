Jixin Zhang

Associate Professor, Ph.D.

Dr. Jixin Zhang is an associate professor at the School of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, Hubei University of Technology. He is a Visiting Scholar at The Chinese University of Hong Kong. He is a technical expert for multiple enterprises. His research interests cover fundamental artificial intelligence methods, AI security, and interdisciplinary AI applications (electronic design, intelligent security, etc.). He led more than 10 industrial R&D projects for Huawei and other companies, and his academic honors include the Huawei Spark Award, the First Prize of Science and Technology Progress Award of China General Chamber of Commerce, and the Second Prize of Science and Technology Progress Award of China Institute of Communications. He has published over 60 papers in international conferences and journals, such as IEEE-TIFS, IEEE-TCAD, IEEE-TDSC, IEEE-TACL, AAAI, IJCAI and DATE etc. He has supervised student teams to win over 20 national competition awards, including the National Grand Prize of the "Challenge Cup", and more than 10 first prizes at the national level in other discipline competitions.
