Hua Shen

Professor, Ph.D.

Dr. Hua Shen is a Professor at the Institute of Cyberspace Security at HBUT. She obtained her Ph.D. from Wuhan University in 2014 and was a visiting scholar at the University of Wollongong. She is a senior member of the China Computer Federation and a member of IEEE.
