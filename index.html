INSTITUTE OF CYBERSPACE SECURITY

The institute brings together faculty and graduate students working on cryptographic mechanisms and their use in securing data, computation, and intelligent systems. It is led by Professor Mingwu Zhang.

Research Areas

Public-key & Applied Cryptography

Functional and witness encryption, identity-based encryption, leakage resilience, and provably secure program obfuscation.

Big-data Security & Privacy

Secure data sharing, verifiable statistics, privacy-preserving aggregation, and outsourced computation.

Blockchain & Industrial Internet

Blockchain supervision, secure crowdsensing, fair electronic auctions, and Internet of Things privacy.

Artificial Intelligence Security

Privacy-preserving federated learning, secure model evaluation, and protected computation on sensitive datasets.
